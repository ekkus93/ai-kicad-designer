# Drawings centred on the page (2026-11-12)

The designs were generated by `NativeSchematicEngine._compile` under brief v5 (`fixtures/pivot/schematic_review_brief_v5.json`), from the working tree committed at `3dc5252c`. They were assessed with `NativeSchematicEngine.assess` on a Linux host, which is not the locked qualification host, using KiCad 10.0.6 via `/tmp/AppDir/AppRun`.

**Problem:** the compiler placed every drawing against the top-left of the frame. On the 15 accepted drawings, the drawing's centre sat between −0.10 and −0.75 of the frame's half-width from the frame centre, and between +0.02 and −0.81 of its half-height. The qualification's "off-balance page" injection only moved a drawing to the top-right corner, so it was no worse than the accepted output, and nothing caught it (`readability_gates_v4_2026-11-12_run1`). The brief's 65% page-utilization target never failed: drawings use 4–39% of the frame.

**Change (owner choice, 2026-11-12):**
- **Centring:** as the last presentation step, the whole drawing is translated to the centre of the usable frame (`centre_on_page_text`). The move is a whole number of 1.27 mm grid steps, so every connection stays on grid. Offsets are tried nearest exact centring first, and the first that keeps the drawing inside the frame and clear of the title block is used. It is recorded in `presentation.json` as `page_centring`.
- **The gate:** `page_balance`, a brief-v5 independent gate in `readability_checks.py`. It measures the box around every wire, symbol body and visible field or label text. The box must lie inside the usable frame (x 20.32–276.68 mm, y 25.4–184.6 mm) and clear of the title block. Its centre may sit at most 0.25 of the half-width and half-height from the frame centre.
- **Brief v5:** v4 plus `page_balance`. The unmeasured utilization target is dropped. Centring runs only under v5, so v3 and v4 drawings are unchanged.

**Results:**
- All 15 corpus designs were regenerated. Every check passes, including ERC, electrical equivalence, the protected layout observer, the three v4 gates and `page_balance`.
- Each drawing's centre is now within 0.007 of the frame centre on both axes. The moves range from (12.7, −1.27) mm for noninverting_gain to (96.52, 55.88) mm for led_long_text.
- Every drawing is exactly its accepted drawing from `review_loop_corpus_v4_2026-11-12_qwen35_256k_run1`, translated by the recorded offset. Nothing else changed (compared with UUIDs stripped).
- The three op-amp connector drawings no longer extend past the left frame edge: their text used to reach 7.2 mm beyond it.
- Each design folder has `page_before.png`, the v4 run's accepted page, and `review-images/page.png`, the centred page.
- No model review has been run on these drawings yet.

**Not addressed:** small circuits still fill little of an A4 sheet (led_series uses 4% of the frame). No minimum fill or smaller sheet size has been chosen.
