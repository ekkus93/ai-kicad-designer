# PWR_FLAG turned away from its net label (2026-11-01)

The designs were generated by `NativeSchematicEngine._compile` (brief v3 power-symbol rails) from the working tree committed as `2262ee64`. They were then assessed with `NativeSchematicEngine.assess` on this Linux host (KiCad 10.0.6 via `/tmp/AppDir/AppRun`), which is not the locked qualification host.

**The problem:** on dual_buffer, noninverting_gain and sallen_key, the VMINUS PWR_FLAG on a connector stub shared its connection point with the VMINUS net label. It hung straight down under the label text, 0.254 mm from the VMINUS rail wire.

**The new step:** `aim_power_flags_text` rotates any flag within 1.27 mm of a label's text about its own pin point, to the free direction with the most clearance. Here that is left, angle 90.

**Results:**
- `presentation.json` records each turn under `power_flag_turns`.
- Apart from UUIDs, the only change from the schematics accepted in `review_loop_corpus_v3_2026-11-01_qwen35_32k_run2` is that flag's angle.
- `assessment.json`: every independent check passes for all three designs.
- `sallen_key/j1_closeup_before.png` and `j1_closeup_after.png` are 10x crops of the J1 area. The "before" crop is from run2's render.
- No model review has been run on these.
