They were then assessed with `NativeSchematicEngine.assess` on this Linux host (KiCad 10.0.6 via `/tmp/AppDir/AppRun`), which is not the locked qualification host.

**Request:** an outside (ChatGPT) review of the v8 inverting_gain (`power_block_v8_2026-11-13_run1`) preferred v8 to v7. It flagged the VMINUS wire, which rose from U1C pin 4 to C2's top at C1's height, as an unnecessary detour, and asked to "favor electrical readability over rigid symmetry" by placing C2 so the wire is short. The owner directed that change. ChatGPT's other fix, VMINUS symbols at pin 4 and C2 with no wire, is rejected by the protected layout observer, which requires an explicit decoupling wire.

**Changes (v9 only; v8 and earlier drawings are unchanged):**
- **Bottom capacitor placement.** The power block keeps the bottom pin's capacitor at the mirrored distance from the unit's pin line, but places its rail pin level with the bottom pin's end. Its wire is one straight run, and the capacitors are no longer at the same height.
- **Flag on a decoupling wire.** A power flag moved onto a decoupling wire now sits at least three grid steps from the capacitor's pin and tries pointing down at every spot before pointing up. Close to the pin, or pointing up, it blocked the rail's own arrow above the capacitor.
