# Corpus drawings under brief v7 (2026-11-12)

The designs were generated by `NativeSchematicEngine._compile` under brief v7 (`fixtures/pivot/schematic_review_brief_v7.json`) at `62d0bc5c`. They were assessed with `NativeSchematicEngine.assess` on a Linux host, which is not the locked qualification host, using KiCad 10.0.6 via `/tmp/AppDir/AppRun`. No model review was run.

**Change (owner request, 2026-11-12):** brief v7 is brief v6 plus the following.
- **U1C's label:** the text of a unit drawn without a body (U1C) is now measured by `field_nearest_owner` against its pin column. The compiler may now write that text beside the column on either side, up to two grid steps off its centre line, and it rejects a move that leaves text nearer another part than its own. Before, it tried only the right side, level with the centre.
- **`field_wire_separation`:** a part's reference or value fails when the straight line from its text to the nearest point of its own drawing meets a wire of another net. Wiring joined to the part's own pins does not count.
- **`ground_symbol_direction`:** a ground glyph fails when it points sideways although pointing down would neither point into its own pin nor touch another drawn item, at any value-text spot the compiler tries. Supply arrows may still point sideways (VPLUS on pin 8, owner decision).
