# Net label clear of connector pin numbers (2026-11-01)

The designs were generated by `NativeSchematicEngine._compile` (brief v3 power-symbol rails) from the working tree committed as `4e9c8888`. They were then assessed with `NativeSchematicEngine.assess` on this Linux host (KiCad 10.0.6 via `/tmp/AppDir/AppRun`), which is not the locked qualification host.

**The problem:** on the op-amp connectors, the end of the J1 VMINUS label text was 0.14 mm from pin number 4 (the observer's text estimate).

**The new step, `clear_label_pin_numbers_text`:**
- It applies to any horizontal label closer than 0.635 mm to a pin number or a symbol field, when the label sits on a free stub end.
- It lengthens the stub outward one 1.27 mm step at a time, moving the label and any PWR_FLAG on that end with it. It stops at the first step where the text is clear and the new stub touches no other wire, pin or body.
- `aim_power_flags_text` then turns flags out of the column above or below a label's text.
- `presentation.json` records `label_moves` and `power_flag_turns`.

**Results:**
- The VMINUS label moved 1.27 mm on dual_buffer, noninverting_gain, rc_to_buffer and sallen_key. The new gap to pin number 4 is 1.41 mm.
- inverting_gain is unchanged. The VPLUS rail runs one grid step outside its VMINUS stub, so a longer stub would short the rails. The first version of the step did exactly that, and ERC and the connectivity check caught it.
- On linear_regulator, the VIN flag now points left, beside its label, instead of hanging under it.
- `assessment.json`: every independent check passes for all six designs.
- No model review has been run on these.
