# Brief v8 layout: balanced decoupling block, power flags at entry (2026-11-13)

The designs were generated by `NativeSchematicEngine._compile` with the brief-v7 checks plus the v8 layout (`power_block=True`), from the working tree committed with this README. They were then assessed with `NativeSchematicEngine.assess` on this Linux host (KiCad 10.0.6 via `/tmp/AppDir/AppRun`), which is not the locked qualification host.

**Request:** an outside (ChatGPT) review of inverting_gain from `review_loop_corpus_v7_2026-11-13_qwen35_256k_run3` asked for three presentation fixes. The owner directed that its rules override earlier owner choices:
1. A standard power section: C1 and C2 at the same height either side of U1C, rails above and the return below.
2. All PWR_FLAGs near J1, where power enters.
3. U1C's reference and value above the power unit.

Its electrical points (a 9.1 kΩ bias resistor, supply voltages, footprints) concern the fixed test design, not its drawing, and were not changed. The protected layout observer still requires each decoupling capacitor to be wired to its pin, so the block keeps those wires.

**Changes (brief v8, `fixtures/pivot/schematic_review_brief_v8.json`; v3–v7 drawings are unchanged):**
- **Power block** (`balance_power_block_text`). For an op-amp power unit whose top and bottom pins are both decoupling pairs, the bottom pin's capacitor is moved to mirror the top pin's capacitor across the unit, at the same height. Its wire to the bottom pin is redrawn as a Z that rises midway between the pin line and the capacitor. Both capacitors must already have their rail pin on top; only translation is supported.
- **Power flags at entry** (`gather_power_flags_text`). A flag moves to the connector pin where its rail enters: on that pin's wire, or up to three grid steps past its free end. It must point across the wire, so it never takes the free end a rail symbol needs. It must be 1.27 mm clear of everything, and the protected layout check must accept it. Otherwise a flag on a decoupling capacitor's rail pin moves onto that capacitor's own wire, freeing the pin. If no rail plan is valid with the flags at the connector, planning is repeated with flags moved only along their wires, then with flags unmoved.
- **Supply arrows** point up wherever they fit (as grounds point down), except at a connector. While symbols are placed, the power unit's own text is ignored, since it is placed afterwards, and rail symbols keep 1.27 mm from net labels.
- **U1C's label** goes above the unit through the v7 placement, which tries above first, once VPLUS no longer sits on pin 8.

**Results:**
- All 15 corpus designs were regenerated, and every independent check passes for all of them, including the v4–v7 gates.
- Six changed. `drawing_before.png` is the v7 run3 drawing.
  - **inverting_gain:** the full block. C1 and C2 mirror each other 10.16 mm either side of U1C, with VPLUS and VMINUS pointing up and REF below both. C2's wire to pin 4 is a Z rising midway. "U1C / NE5532" sits above pin 8.
  - **Flags at J1:** the VMINUS and REF flags were already at J1. The VPLUS flag cannot fit there: J1's VPLUS row lies 2.54 mm between the OUT label and the VMINUS row, and its free end is the only place J1's VPLUS arrow fits. It sits on C1's wire to pin 8 instead.
  - **dual_buffer, dual_rail_reference, noninverting_gain, rc_to_buffer:** the VPLUS flag moved off C1's pin onto C1's wire, the same J1 limit applying. Their C2 is drawn with the rail pin at the bottom, so the block (translation only) does not apply, and the power section keeps its v7 layout.
  - **reference_555_led_flasher_v8:** the REF flag moved from C1 to the wire by J3 ("Return"), where REF enters.
- led_long_text, led_reverse_supply, led_series, linear_regulator, rc_highpass, rc_lowpass, sallen_key, timer_astable and timer_astable_gnd are identical to v7 run3 apart from UUIDs. sallen_key's flag moves were all undone because no rail plan was valid with them.
