# Op-amp power unit text kept above its top pin (2026-11-11)

The designs were generated by `NativeSchematicEngine._compile` (brief v3 power-symbol rails) from the working tree committed with this README. They were then assessed with `NativeSchematicEngine.assess` on this Linux host (KiCad 10.0.6 via `/tmp/AppDir/AppRun`), which is not the locked qualification host.

**Problem:** in four drawings accepted by `review_loop_corpus_v3_2026-11-11_qwen35_256k_run2`, "U1C / NE5532" sat to the right of the NE5532 power unit, beside its V+/V− pin names: dual_rail_reference, inverting_gain, rc_to_buffer and sallen_key. The owner asked for it back above pin 8. Field tightening offered a power unit spots beside it or above it and took the nearest clear one. In inverting_gain and sallen_key, the spot beside the unit became clear once `9a2a2d13` stopped drawing ground symbols sideways there.

**Change:** a power unit's reference and value now go just above its top pin. The search reaches up to 5.08 mm above the pin, and the text may shift sideways by up to its own half-width plus 1.27 mm, so it can sit beside a wire that leaves the pin upward. The spot beside the unit is used only if nothing above fits. Without the 5.08 mm limit, rc_to_buffer's text climbed about 20 mm, past two wires, because its pin-8 wire runs straight up.

**Results:**
- The six op-amp designs were regenerated, and every independent check passes. `assessment.json` is included for the four that changed. `closeup_before.png` (from run2) and `closeup_after.png` show U1C in each.
- inverting_gain, dual_rail_reference and sallen_key: the text is centred above pin 8.
- rc_to_buffer: the text is above pin 8, just left of the VPLUS wire.
- dual_buffer and noninverting_gain already had their text above pin 8 and are identical to run2 apart from UUIDs.
- The other nine corpus designs have no power unit, and this code path does not affect them.
- No model review has been run on these drawings yet.
