# Blinded M2c2 review packet

Two electronics-experienced reviewers are required; at least one must not have authored the layout rule. Review each sheet independently at full-sheet and readable PDF scale. The schematics are unedited KiCad SVG exports; PDFs are direct vector conversions with unchanged geometry and text.

For each sheet, complete the matching score sheet before receiving the separate answer sheet. Record trace time, severe defects, and 1–5 ratings. A case passes human review only when neither reviewer reports a severe defect, every rating dimension has median at least 4, no individual rating is below 3, and the trace answers are correct.

This packet includes only the two successfully generated held-out sheets. Six other requested designs failed the automated gate and have no rendered sheet for review. The separate evaluation mapping is withheld from reviewers.
